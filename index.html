Example colour shown. Your captures appear here.

Each capture averages the patch under the crosshair over several video frames. Phone cameras adjust white balance and exposure, so readings are close but not exact. For best results use even daylight and fill the crosshair with the colour. A phone light helps in dim rooms but is a cool white, so it can tint readings blue and glare on glossy surfaces; tilt the phone if you see a bright spot.
